CS-01, 2023.09 - Present

Jetson autonomous computing platform

From boot to runtime on custom Orin boards. I traced a camera failure after seven hours of streaming to the vendor kernel's VI5 capture path.

  • Patched two kernel defects, a busy loop and a DMA leak; verified 66 hours without failure
  • Traced and prevented apt updates overwriting the custom kernel and DTB
  • Standardized field deployment with a patch tool that needs no reflash
  • Fixed three Ethernet switch defects (zero loss under 1 Gbps saturation for 30 minutes) and removed a 205-second camera boot delay

CS-02, 2025.03 - Present

Jetson BSP port to Yocto

I moved a large, inconsistent Ubuntu-based JetPack BSP to Yocto with meta-tegra. The recipes define a reproducible image for the whole team.

  • One custom layer for Orin NX and AGX Orin, with machine-specific overrides
  • 67% less disk use, 86% fewer packages and 79% less idle memory than JetPack, measured on the same board
  • BSP documentation and onboarding guides for engineers new to Yocto
  • Systemd startup 62 to 6.2 seconds; NVMe detected in 19/19 boots; traced a latent failure after three reboots

SUGV Component Manager

Sensors connect and disconnect while the platform is running. I designed runtime management that handles hot-plug events without restarting the system.

How I work

My approach

  • Investigate at the kernel and driver level
  • Account for NVIDIA update policy changesPrevent JetPack OTA from overwriting custom kernels and DTBs
  • Follow the cause through to a fix

Leave a clear record

  • Write Yocto onboarding guides and BSP documentation
  • Document investigations so the team can follow the reasoning
  • Keep a change history that others can pick up

Use AI, verify the results

  • Use AI for log analysis, repeated research and document drafts

  • Base documentation and patches on checked results
